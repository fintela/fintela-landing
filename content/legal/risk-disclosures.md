## General Risk Notice

Fintela is a software platform for designing, optimizing, and analyzing investment strategies. Fintela is not a registered investment adviser, broker-dealer, or securities market intermediary in any jurisdiction; it does not custody funds, securities, or crypto-assets, and does not execute trades on its own account. Nothing on the platform constitutes investment advice, an offer, a solicitation, or a personalized recommendation.

Instruments accessible through the platform — including stocks, ETFs, forex, and cryptocurrencies — carry risk of loss. Trading these instruments may result in the total loss of invested capital and is not suitable for all users.

These notices are general and do not replace the risk warnings or regulatory documentation that the broker, exchange, or execution/custody provider a user works with must provide under the rules applicable in that user's jurisdiction.

## Simulated and Hypothetical Results

1. **Hypothetical nature.** Backtests, optimizations, and simulations generated on Fintela are hypothetical. They do not represent real trades or capital actually committed, and do not reflect the performance of any account.
2. **Inherent limitations.** Simulated results are built with hindsight and may not reflect available liquidity, slippage, transaction costs, or real execution conditions.
3. **Overfitting risk.** Optimizing parameters on historical data can produce configurations fitted to past conditions that may not hold out-of-sample or in live trading.

## Forex

1. **Leverage and margin.** Many forex trades use leverage, which amplifies both gains and losses. Even a small adverse move can cause a significant loss relative to deposited capital, forced closure of positions for insufficient margin, and — depending on broker terms and applicable regulation — losses exceeding the initial deposit.
2. **Volatility.** Exchange rates can move rapidly and unpredictably due to economic events, central bank decisions, geopolitical conditions, or other factors outside Fintela's control.
3. **Counterparty risk.** Execution and custody are handled by a regulated third-party provider in the relevant jurisdiction, not by Fintela.
4. **Varying regulatory framework.** Leverage limits, retail-client protections, and disclosure obligations vary by country and regulator. Users are responsible for knowing and complying with the rules of their jurisdiction of residence.

## Cryptocurrencies and Digital Assets

1. **Custody risk.** Fintela does not custody crypto-assets. Depending on where assets are held, users may be exposed to insolvency, security breaches, or mismanagement by whoever controls the private keys or funds.
2. **Security and fraud risk.** The crypto-asset market has been subject to phishing, malware, identity theft, fraudulent schemes, and price manipulation.
3. **Regulatory risk.** The applicable legal framework varies significantly across countries and can change without notice, including new restrictions, bans, or compliance obligations. Such changes can affect the value, availability, or legality of certain transactions.
4. **Liquidity risk.** Some crypto-assets, particularly those with lower market capitalization or volume, can be difficult to buy or sell without affecting their price.
5. **Lack of traditional protections.** Unlike bank deposits or regulated securities accounts, crypto-assets generally carry no government insurance or the investor-protection mechanisms available for other financial products.

---

## Stocks and ETFs

1. **Market risk.** The value of stocks and ETFs fluctuates and can fall below the purchase price, with no guarantee of recovering invested capital.
2. **Issuer risk.** A stock's performance depends on the issuer's financial, operational, and competitive position, as well as sector and macroeconomic factors.
3. **ETF-specific risk.** An ETF may deviate from the index or asset it tracks (tracking error) and may trade at a premium or discount to its net asset value.
4. **Liquidity risk.** Securities with low market capitalization or trading volume can be difficult to sell promptly without affecting their price.
